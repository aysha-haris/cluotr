Product roundups are ideal for **Pinterest collections** and seasonal traffic spikes.

## Roundup structure

- Lead with a pin-worthy hero image
- Group products by use case or aesthetic
- Include clear affiliate CTAs with disclosure

Writers can add new roundups in `src/content/roundups/` without touching application code.
